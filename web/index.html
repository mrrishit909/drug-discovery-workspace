Discovery Workspace

Ten thousand invented molecules, one invented target with a hidden binding rule, three hundred measured. Which 96 go on the next plate, how sure is the model, and can a chemist trace every recommendation back to measured neighbours? …
The library, the target and every measurement are generated by a simulator in this repository; the molecules are real structures handled by RDKit, but the target and its binding rule are invented. Research decision support on synthetic data, nothing clinical. No outside model or service is called.
